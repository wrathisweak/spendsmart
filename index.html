Help Center

How do I set my budget?

Go to the setup screen or profile settings to set your monthly spending limit. You can set it manually or let the app recommend based on your income.

How do I add expenses?

Tap the "+ Add" button at the bottom navigation, select a category, enter the amount, and save. The expense will be added to your tracker.

What are alternative products?

When you click on a product in "For You", we show cheaper alternatives to help you save money while still getting what you need.

How do I change my profile picture?

Go to your Profile screen and click on your profile picture. You can upload a new image or use a default avatar.
